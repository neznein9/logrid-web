Logic Grid Puzzles

Enjoy classic logic-grid puzzles (sometimes called Einstein or Zebra puzzles) with a modern design and smarter approach to solving. Every puzzle is handcrafted, carefully tested, and designed to be solved through logical reasoning and deduction. No guessing required!

Learn As You Solve

If you get stuck, spoiler-free nudges show you where to look next without revealing answers. Or ask for a hint and Logrid's intelligent hint system can explain the next logical move forward with intent and reasoning. Learn new techniques, sharpen your reasoning skills, and become a better solver with every puzzle.
