    # Logrid Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 19, 2026
**Publisher:** Sampson Interactive LLC

This policy describes how Sampson Interactive LLC handles information in **Logrid**, our puzzle game for iOS and iPadOS.

## Information we collect

Logrid does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, or other contact information to play.

We use **Amplitude Analytics** to understand game usage and improve Logrid. Analytics include gameplay events (such as puzzle starts and completions), app sessions, app lifecycle events, app version, and basic device and operating-system information.

For analytics, Logrid generates a random installation identifier (UUID) on your device and stores it locally. The identifier is reused across app launches so we can understand usage over time. We do not send an account user ID or Apple's advertising or vendor identifier to Amplitude. Logrid does not collect IP addresses or geographic location information as analytics data.

Logrid does not display ads, sell personal information, or use analytics for cross-app advertising tracking.

## How information is used and shared

We use analytics to understand player engagement, improve puzzles and features, and assess app performance. Amplitude processes analytics information on our behalf. Analytics records are subject to a **24-month retention setting** in Amplitude.

Logrid supports optional **Apple Game Center achievements**. You can play without signing in to Game Center. Apple's handling of Game Center information is governed by Apple's privacy practices.

Game progress is stored locally on your device. Game settings are stored using Apple’s iCloud storage service. This data is managed by Apple under its privacy policies. Apple may also separately collect app diagnostics through its own services and device settings.

## Support and privacy requests

For questions about privacy or requests concerning analytics information, contact **support@logrid.app**. Because we do not maintain player accounts, we may need information that helps identify the relevant installation to locate its analytics records.

## Children's privacy

Logrid is a general-audience puzzle game suitable for a range of ages. It does not require personal contact information, contain advertising, or use behavioral advertising. Its limited analytics practices are described above. Parents and guardians may contact us with privacy questions or requests.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Logrid changes. Any revised policy will include a new effective date.
